# CStatic's Art Fight HTML Templates 
A GitHub mirror of all of the HTML templates I've made for [Art Fight](https://artfight.net/). Everything from my [Toyhou.se](https://toyhou.se/CStatic) should be here too. As always, these were made specifically for Art Fight and may not work on Toyhou.se or other sites.

You are free to adapt, change, and translate any of my templates to suit your needs. 

## How to Use
Copy and paste the raw files into your editor of choice. I would recommend keeping a backup of all of your files before and after editing the code with [Circlejourney](https://af.circlejourney.net) in case the Art Fight website sanitizes and deletes parts of your code.
